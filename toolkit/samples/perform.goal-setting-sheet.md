## My goals for October 2026 to September 2027

### Goal 1: Grow Luzon general trade

- **Goal:** Grow Luzon general-trade revenue by 20% against the previous year.
- **Measure and target:** Revenue growth against October 2025 to September 2026, +20%.
- **Deadline:** September 30, 2027.
- **Quarterly milestones:** Q1 sales plan agreed and all 14 key distributors visited · Q2 +8% year to date · Q3 +14% year to date · Q4 +20%.
- **Support I need:** The monthly promotion calendar from marketing, agreed by the 15th of the month before.

### Goal 2: Collect faster

- **Goal:** Cut days-sales-outstanding from 62 to 45 days.
- **Measure and target:** Average days-sales-outstanding, 45 days by month 12.
- **Deadline:** September 30, 2027.
- **Quarterly milestones:** Q1 58 days, and ₱3M of receivables older than 90 days recovered · Q2 54 days · Q3 49 days · Q4 45 days.
- **Support I need:** A weekly aging report from Finance, and agreed rules for holding supply.

### Goal 3: Build a team that hits target

- **Goal:** Coach all 8 sales officers to hit their monthly target.
- **Measure and target:** Officers meeting their monthly target, 8 of 8 by month 12.
- **Deadline:** September 30, 2027.
- **Quarterly milestones:** Q1 5 of 8, all with written goals · Q2 6 of 8 · Q3 7 of 8 · Q4 8 of 8.
- **Support I need:** A clear process with HR for any officer still below target after a 60-day improvement plan.

### Goal 4: Hold every performance conversation

- **Goal:** Hold a monthly performance conversation with every sales officer.
- **Measure and target:** 100% of conversations held and logged every month.
- **Deadline:** Every month, starting November 2026.
- **Quarterly milestones:** Q1 conversation log set up and the first round complete · Q2 to Q4 100% every month.
- **Support I need:** 30 minutes protected in each officer's calendar every month.

## Development goal

- **Goal:** Learn to lead managers, not only sales officers, ready for nationwide expansion.
- **Measure and target:** Complete the Lead Others level of the SPARK leadership program, and name one officer as a successor in development.
- **Deadline:** September 30, 2027.
- **Support I need:** An Individual Development Plan under the ACCELERATE pillar, agreed with the COO in Q1.

## How we will review progress

- A 30-minute performance conversation with the COO every month, using the Performance Scorecard.
- A formal mid-year review in April 2027.
- A year-end review in September 2027 against the Performance Scorecard.
