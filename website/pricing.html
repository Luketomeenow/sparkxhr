Questions

Before you decide.

Anything else, ask Fides directly on a 30-minute call.

Is the Pulse Check really free?

Yes. There is no payment and no card. You answer fifteen questions, receive your report by email, and Fides may follow up to offer a 30-minute call. You are free to say no.

Do you replace our payroll or HRIS?

No. SPARKXHR is about judgment, process, and people decisions. Keep your payroll and HR software. Where you need one, we refer you to providers such as Sprout or Titanium rather than competing with them.

Can we start with just one pillar?

Yes, and most companies do. Buy the toolkit for your weakest pillar, or run a two-to-six week Sprint on it. The Pulse Check tells you which pillar to start with.

How is SPARK Partner different from hiring a CHRO?

You get the same level of judgment at less than a fifth of the cost, starting in week one instead of after a long search. Each quarter focuses on one pillar, and the CEO scorecard shows the peso impact.

What if we don't see results?

If you don't see a clear path to ROI in the first 30 days, we part ways. There is no long-term lock-in.

How is our data protected?

Personal data is handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012. The AI in SPARKXHR runs under provider terms that prohibit using your data to train public models, and each client's data is kept separate. See the privacy notice.
