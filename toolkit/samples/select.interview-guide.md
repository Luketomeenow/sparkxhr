## Before the day

- Confirm the room, the times, and lunch for the candidate.

## Attitude interview

**Ownership: Tell me about a sales target you missed. What did you do in the following 30 days?**
**Listen for:** a specific target, what they personally changed, and what happened next.
**Red flag:** blames the market, the product, or the team without naming their own part.

**Coaching: Tell me about the weakest performer on a team you led. What did you do, and what happened?**
**Listen for:** time spent developing the person, a clear plan, and an honest outcome, including a separation if it came to that.
**Red flag:** replaced the person without trying to develop them, or quietly did their work for them.

**Straight talk: Describe a time you disagreed with your boss about a target or a decision. How did you raise it?**
**Listen for:** raised it directly and with facts, then committed fully once the decision was made.
**Red flag:** stayed silent and complained later, or went around the boss.

## Capability interview

**Growing Luzon general-trade revenue by 20%: Walk me through your first 90 days. Where would you look first for growth?**
**Listen for:** starts with data and distributor visits, names specific levers such as coverage, distribution, pricing, and promotions, and puts them in order.
**Follow-up:** Which areas would you focus on, and what would you stop doing?

**Building and coaching a team of 8 sales officers: How would you set up your team in the first month?**
**Listen for:** individual targets, a weekly rhythm, field coaching, and monthly performance conversations.
**Follow-up:** How would you decide which officers to invest in and which to replace?

**Cutting days-sales-outstanding from 62 to 45: What would you do about receivables older than 90 days?**
**Listen for:** works with Finance, agrees terms up front, holds supply when limits are breached, and protects the relationship.
**Follow-up:** Tell me about a difficult account you collected without losing the customer.

## Work sample

The candidate presents "My first 30 days as Sales Manager at Bayanihan Foods" in 20 minutes, followed by 10 minutes of questions. They receive the Job Description and a one-page summary of the current Luzon sales position the working day before. Slides are optional. A clear plan is not.

The panel assesses:

- Whether the plan starts from the three outcomes or from activities.
- Whether the priorities are realistic for a 140-person company and in the right order.
- How the candidate responds when the plan is challenged.

## Peer conversation

- "How do you like to work with key account and modern-trade colleagues?"
- "When a promotion or a delivery goes wrong, what do you do first?"
- "What would your last team say you are like in a bad week?"

## Scoring rubric

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 4 · Strong yes | Specific example, clear personal role, measurable result, honest about what went wrong |
| 3 · Yes | Specific example and result, personal role mostly clear |
| 2 · Doubtful | General or borrowed example, vague result, no learning |
| 1 · No | No example, or an example that contradicts our values |

## Decision rule

Hire only when at least two interviewers score "Strong yes" on attitude and nobody scores "No" on any dimension. Capability gaps can be trained. Attitude gaps cannot. Every interviewer completes their scorecard before the huddle, and the most junior interviewer speaks first.

## Candidate experience
