# 90-Day New Hire Success Check

*Bayanihan Foods Corporation · Sales Manager · New hire: Carlo Dizon · Manager: A. Cruz, Chief Operating Officer · Start date: October 19, 2026*

## Why we do this

A new hire who is set up to win in 90 days stays. One left to figure it out tends to leave after the business has already paid for the whole probation.

## Check-in schedule

| Check-in | Focus | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 · Wednesday, November 18, 2026 | Understand the role, the numbers, and the people | A. Cruz with Carlo |
| Day 60 · Friday, December 18, 2026 | Agree the 12-month plan and show first results | A. Cruz with Carlo |
| Day 90 · Monday, January 18, 2027 | Visible results and the outlook for regularization | A. Cruz, Carlo, and HR |

## Part C: Questions for the new hire

- What has surprised you most about the role or the company?
- Where are you spending time that is not moving the three outcomes?
- What is one thing we should change about how we onboarded you?
- What do you need from your manager in the next 90 days?
