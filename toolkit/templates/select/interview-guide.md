Purpose: an Interview Guide for the SPARK 1-Day Hiring Process. Every interviewer uses the same questions and the same scoring, and the panel decides on the same day.

Length: 700 to 1,000 words.

Sections, in this order:

1. `# Interview Guide: [Role title]` then the metadata line: company · 1-Day Hiring Process · hiring manager · interview date if given.
2. `## The day at a glance`: a table with columns Time | Session | Who. Sessions: attitude interview (HR), capability interview (hiring manager), work sample presentation, peer conversation, decision huddle, verbal decision to the candidate. Use the panel names if given, otherwise roles. Finish by mid-afternoon.
3. `## Before the day`: four or five checklist bullets: CVs screened against the must-haves, the work sample brief sent to the candidate in advance, scorecards printed, panel briefed on the outcomes, room and times confirmed.
4. `## Attitude interview`: three questions, one per attitude trait. For each question use this format: the question in bold, then **Listen for:** one sentence, then **Red flag:** one sentence.
5. `## Capability interview`: one question per outcome, each asking how the candidate would approach it in their first 90 days, plus one follow-up probe. Same bold question format, with **Listen for:**.
6. `## Work sample`: a short brief (three or four sentences) for a 20-minute presentation tied to the most important outcome, sent the day before. List two or three things the panel should assess.
7. `## Peer conversation`: three prompts the future colleague can use, focused on how the candidate works with others.
8. `## Scoring rubric`: a table with columns Score | Meaning, for scores 4 (Strong yes), 3 (Yes), 2 (Doubtful), 1 (No), each with a one-line anchor about evidence quality.
9. `## Decision rule`: hire only when at least two interviewers score "Strong yes" on attitude and nobody scores "No" on any dimension. Add the rule that each interviewer completes their scorecard before the huddle, and the most junior interviewer speaks first.
10. `## Candidate experience`: two or three bullets: same-day verbal decision, written offer within [number] working days, thank-you message to candidates who are not selected, and that candidate data is handled under the Data Privacy Act.
11. `## Review notes`
