Purpose: the Goal-Setting Sheet. It gives one person written goals with a clear line of sight from company priorities to their own work. It is agreed in a conversation, not handed down.

Length: 400 to 650 words.

Sections, in this order:

1. `# Goal-Setting Sheet: [Employee name or role]` then the metadata line: company · role · manager · goal period.
2. `## Line of sight`: a table with columns Company priority | Team goal | My goal. One row per company priority from the inputs, two to four rows, with the individual goal written as a short phrase.
3. `## My goals for [period]`: three to five goals. For each goal use a `###` heading "Goal [n]: [short name]" and then these bullets: **Goal:** one sentence, verb-first; **Measure and target:** specific; **Deadline:**; **Quarterly milestones:** Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 in one line each or a compact list; **Support I need:** one line.
4. `## Development goal`: one goal that builds a capability the role needs next, linked to an Individual Development Plan in the ACCELERATE pillar. Same bullet format, without quarterly milestones.
5. `## How we will review progress`: three bullets: monthly performance conversation, formal mid-year review, year-end review against the Performance Scorecard.
6. `## Agreement`: one sentence that both parties agreed these goals in conversation, then a table with columns Name | Role | Signature | Date for the employee and the manager.
7. `## Review notes`
