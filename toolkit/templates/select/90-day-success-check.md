Purpose: the 90-Day New Hire Success Check, the proof layer of SELECT. It confirms each hire is working before probation ends, gives the new hire a voice, and triggers support early.

Length: 450 to 700 words.

Sections, in this order:

1. `# 90-Day New Hire Success Check` then the metadata line: company · role · new hire (or [Name]) · manager · start date if given.
2. `## Why we do this`: two sentences. A new hire set up to win in 90 days stays; one left to figure it out leaves after the business has paid for the whole probation.
3. `## Check-in schedule`: a table with columns Check-in | Focus | Owner, with rows Day 30 (understand the role and the people), Day 60 (early plan and first results), Day 90 (visible results and regularization outlook). Add weekly one-to-ones as a line below the table.
4. `## Part A: Ratings`: a table with columns Statement | New hire (1-5) | Manager (1-5). Six statements covering role clarity, early results, manager support, tools and access, team fit with the company values, and intent to stay. Leave the score cells blank for completion. State the scale: 1 strongly disagree to 5 strongly agree.
5. `## Part B: Progress on the 90-day goals`: a table with columns Goal | Status (On track / Behind / Ahead) | Evidence. One row per goal from the inputs, with Status and Evidence left blank.
6. `## Part C: Questions for the new hire`: three or four open questions, including what surprised them and one thing to change about onboarding.
7. `## Part D: Support for the next 90 days`: two or three blank prompts for the manager to commit specific support.
8. `## Part E: Decision`: a table with columns Decision | What happens next, with three rows: Confirm on track for regularization; Extend support plan (named gaps, 30-day plan, re-check); Recommend separation before probation ends (HR confirms the regularization standards were communicated at engagement and that due process is followed before any decision).
9. `## Sign-off`: a table with columns Name | Role | Signature | Date for the new hire, the manager, and HR.
10. `## Review notes`
