## Rating scale

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Well above target, and raised the bar for others. |
| 4 | Exceeds | Above target on most measures. |
| 3 | Meets | Hit the target. Solid, reliable delivery. |
| 2 | Partly meets | Missed some targets. Needs a clear improvement plan. |
| 1 | Does not meet | Missed most targets. Needs urgent action. |

## Overall score

Overall = (Part A weighted average × 70%) + (Part B average × 30%).

Example: a Part A weighted average of 3.8 and a Part B average of 4.0 gives (3.8 × 0.7) + (4.0 × 0.3) = 2.66 + 1.20 = 3.86.
