Purpose: the Performance Scorecard used for monthly performance conversations and the year-end review. Results and behaviors are both scored, so people are measured on what they deliver and how.

Length: 450 to 700 words, mostly tables.

Sections, in this order:

1. `# Performance Scorecard: [Role title]` then the metadata line: company · employee (or [Name]) · manager · review period.
2. `## How to use this scorecard`: three bullets: update progress in a monthly conversation of 30 minutes; score formally at mid-year and year-end; scores feed performance-related rewards and are calibrated across managers using the Calibration Guide.
3. `## Part A: Results (weight [results weight]%)`: a table with columns KPI | Measure | Target | Weight | Mid-year | Year-end | Rating (1-5). One row per KPI from the inputs, three to five rows. Weights within Part A add up to 100%. Leave Mid-year, Year-end, and Rating blank.
4. `## Part B: Behaviors (weight [behavior weight]%)`: a table with columns Value | What it looks like here | Rating (1-5). Use the company values when given, otherwise the role's attitude traits. Each description is one observable behavior. Leave Rating blank.
5. `## Rating scale`: a table with columns Rating | Label | Meaning for 5 Exceptional, 4 Exceeds, 3 Meets, 2 Partly meets, 1 Does not meet. Each meaning is one line in plain language.
6. `## Overall score`: one short worked formula: overall = (Part A average × results weight) + (Part B average × behavior weight). Give a one-line example with sample numbers.
7. `## Monthly conversation log`: a table with columns Month | Progress this month | Support needed | Next step, with twelve blank rows labeled by month for the review period when known, otherwise Month 1 to Month 12.
8. `## Link to rewards`: two sentences on how the overall score informs performance-related rewards under the company's reward framework, using a placeholder for any payout rule not given.
9. `## Review notes`

Default weights when none are given: results 70%, behaviors 30%.
