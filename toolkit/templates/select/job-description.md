Purpose: an outcomes-based Job Description. It tells the candidate what success looks like and becomes the basis of the Performance Scorecard. It is not a list of duties copied from a previous posting.

Length: 450 to 650 words.

Sections, in this order:

1. `# [Role title]` then the metadata line: company · department · reports to · employment type · location.
2. `## About [company short name]`: two or three sentences from the client profile. Do not invent history.
3. `## Why this role exists`: one short paragraph. Name the bottleneck this role removes from the CEO or the business, and what stays stuck if the seat is empty.
4. `## What success looks like in the first 12 months`: the outcomes from the inputs as three to five bullets, each measurable. Tighten wording, keep the numbers.
5. `## Key responsibilities`: six to eight bullets, verb-first, each clearly serving one of the outcomes. Include leading and coaching others if the role manages people.
6. `## Right attitude: what we hire for first`: three traits. Use the company values when given, otherwise infer from the role. Format each as **Trait:** one sentence describing the observable behavior.
7. `## Qualifications`: two short lists, **Must have** and **Good to have**. Must-haves are limited to what the outcomes truly require. Do not require a specific degree unless the inputs say so.
8. `## Compensation and rewards`: the salary band if given, plus performance-related rewards tied to the outcomes, statutory benefits, and when pay is reviewed. Use placeholders for anything not given.
9. `## Probation and regularization`: one short paragraph stating the probationary period of up to six months and that the outcomes above, shared at hiring, are the standards for regularization, reviewed at Day 30, 60, and 90.
10. `## How this role is measured`: a table with columns Measure | Target | Reviewed. One row per outcome. Say in one sentence that these measures carry into the Performance Scorecard.
11. `## Review notes`
