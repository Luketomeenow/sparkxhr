How we handle your data

Last updated September 2026

Who we are

Faith & Possibilities HR Consulting operates SPARKXHR and is the personal information controller for data collected on this website, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

Through the contact form: your name, work email, company, company size, and message. Through the Pulse Check: your company profile, your answers, and your contact details.

Why we collect it

To reply to you, to prepare and email your Pulse Check report, and to follow up about SPARKXHR services you have asked about. We do not sell your data.

AI processing

Pulse Check answers are processed by a third-party AI service provider under commercial terms that prohibit using your data to train publicly available models.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for these purposes, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your data, object to its processing, or request a copy. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise these rights, send a message through the form above.
