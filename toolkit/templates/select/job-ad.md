Purpose: a Job Ad that attracts the right people with the right attitude, fast, and discourages the wrong ones. It sells the outcome and the growth, not the company's size.

Length: 250 to 350 words for the main ad, plus the short social version.

Sections, in this order:

1. `# [Headline]`: benefit-led and specific to the role. Never "We are hiring" or "Job opening". Then the metadata line: company · location · employment type · salary band (write [Salary band] if not given; showing pay is strongly preferred).
2. An opening paragraph with no heading, two or three sentences, speaking directly to the right candidate ("you").
3. `## What you will own`: the outcomes as three to five bullets, in candidate-friendly language.
4. `## What you bring`: three or four bullets. Mix experience with attitude. No age, sex, civil status, religion, height, or appearance requirements.
5. `## What you get`: pay band and performance-related rewards, statutory benefits, and growth (for example a clear 30-60-90 day plan, direct access to leadership). Use placeholders for benefits not given.
6. `## How we hire: one day, not one month`: two or three sentences describing the 1-Day Hiring Process: all interviews on a single day, and a same-day decision.
7. `## How to apply`: the application instruction from the inputs, or [Application email or link]. Ask for a CV plus two or three sentences on the result the applicant is proudest of. End with the line "Open to all qualified applicants."
8. `## Short version for social media`: a 60 to 90 word post for LinkedIn or Facebook, with the headline idea, two outcomes, the pay band, and how to apply. At most three hashtags.
9. `## Review notes`
