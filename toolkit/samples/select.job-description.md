# Sales Manager

*Bayanihan Foods Corporation · Sales & Distribution · Reports to the Chief Operating Officer · Full-time, regular after probation · Pasig City, Metro Manila*

## About Bayanihan Foods

Bayanihan Foods Corporation manufactures and distributes packaged snacks and condiments to general-trade and modern-trade accounts across Luzon. The company has grown to 140 employees and is building the management layer it needs for nationwide distribution.

## Why this role exists

Today the CEO and the COO still carry the general-trade numbers themselves, account by account. That keeps them in the field when they should be building the next stage of the business. The Sales Manager takes ownership of Luzon sales results and the team that delivers them. If this seat stays empty, growth stays capped at the hours two leaders can give it, and overdue receivables keep tying up cash.

## What success looks like in the first 12 months

- Grow Luzon general-trade revenue by 20% against the previous year.
- Build and coach a team of 8 sales officers who each hit their monthly target.
- Cut days-sales-outstanding from 62 to 45 days.

## Key responsibilities

- Own the Luzon general-trade sales plan, targets, and weekly operating rhythm.
- Hire, coach, and hold 8 sales officers to a clear Performance Scorecard.
- Hold a monthly performance conversation with every sales officer.
- Set route and account coverage so every key distributor is visited on a fixed cycle.
- Run collections with Finance: agree credit terms up front, chase overdue accounts, and hold orders when limits are breached.
- Work with modern-trade and marketing colleagues on promotions that move volume, not just shelf space.
- Report results, risks, and decisions needed to the COO every week.
- Build the process so results repeat without heroics.

## Right attitude: what we hire for first

- **Ownership:** treats the Luzon number as a personal commitment and brings a recovery plan, not an excuse, when it slips.
- **Coaching:** measures success by the team's results, and develops officers in the field instead of doing their work for them.
- **Straight talk:** raises problems early, with facts, and gives honest feedback in both directions.

## Qualifications

**Must have**

- At least seven years in FMCG or food sales, including three years leading a sales team.
- A record of hitting targets through others, with the numbers to show it.
- Experience in both general-trade and modern-trade channels.
- Comfort with sales data, dashboards, and weekly reporting.

**Good to have**

- Experience managing credit terms and collections with distributors.
- Existing relationships with Luzon distributors.
